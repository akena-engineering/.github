## Who we are

We are an engineering firm, in operation since 2018. We help founders and
organisations navigate complexity and build systems designed for the long
term. We take ambitious ideas from first principles to production, and we
operate what we build. No hype, just engineering.

## What we build

- **AI agents and MCP** · Tools that give AI agents reliable access to blockchain
  data and infrastructure. See [blockchain-mcp](https://github.com/boar-network/blockchain-mcp).
- **Blockchain infrastructure** · Node operations at scale through
  [Boar Network](https://boar.network). Our nodes secure more than $300M in TVL.
- **Applied cryptography** · Threshold ECDSA systems, including a Bitcoin-Ethereum bridge.
- **Open source** · A browser-based Web3 wallet and public developer tooling.
